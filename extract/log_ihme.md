## Caveats to carry forward

1. **MMR definition**: GBD maternal deaths include late maternal deaths (43-365 days) and HIV-aggravated deaths; not comparable one-to-one with WHO/MMEIG SDG 3.1.1 (42-day) MMR.
2. **TFR**: lower == upper == value in every row; the tool serves no real uncertainty interval for TFR.
3. **NMR / IMR**: no lower/upper columns were returned for the neonatal and <1 bands (custom-band path, no published UI).
4. **Covariates**: 2023 == 2022 for DTP3, MCV1 and MCV2 in all five countries (series appear carried forward). MCV2 Ethiopia and Nigeria 2015-2018 = 0.0 with null bounds (pre-introduction). Contraception covariates (fp_mod, cpr_mod) were served at the "All Ages" label (not the by-age variant).
5. **Stunting/wasting**: GBD publishes three severity categories (mild/moderate/severe) that are a PARTIAL set; the conventional "<-2 SD" prevalence would be series 9+10 (14+15 for wasting) but the tool forbids summing (no interval for a sum). Rows stored per series and age band; there is no under-5 aggregate band and no both-sexes column.
6. **Breastfeeding**: no exclusive-breastfeeding series exists; the three non-exclusive categories are a partial set whose residual (exclusive BF) must not be inferred. Discontinued BF covers 6-11 and 12-23 months only.
7. **IPV**: all_ages aggregate for women, lifetime-exposure framing in GBD; not SDG 5.2.1 (past 12 months).
8. **LBW**: 58 joint birthweight x gestational-age series, exhaustive; no <2500 g total computed. Metadata only.
9. **High BMI**: 4 series across 2 REIs (108 nested: 52 = 53 + 54; 370 adult mean BMI, kg/m2). No child-specific series, but series 52-54 have child bands 2-4, 5-9, 10-14, 15-19 (stored for Nigeria 2022).
10. **Methods**: `search_methods(entity="Child stunting")` found no risk-specific write-up; `entity="Child growth failure"` did (rei 239) and was used for the metadata excerpt.
11. **Usage**: server reports a 50,000-row / 24 h cap; last reading 1,521 rows used (3.0%).
