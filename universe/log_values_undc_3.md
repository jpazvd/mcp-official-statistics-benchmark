## Anomalies

1. NT_BW_LBW: Nigeria has no observations at all, while India and Brazil have full 2000-2020 modeled series.
2. NT_VAS_TWODOSE (NGA): literal 0 in 2000, 2003, 2008 and 2020 among otherwise 31-91 values; likely "no campaign/not reported" coded as zero.
3. SPP_GDPPC, SPP_GINI, SPP_DIST_SOC_PROT_BNFT: date="all" returns a single 2024 point per country; 2024 looks like a release/reference year rather than the survey year (Gini 2024 for Nigeria/India/Brazil is implausible as a survey year).
4. SPP_GINI unit label "undata/UNIT_MEASURE-RATIO" vs 0-100 scale values.
5. JMP facility series (WS_HCF_*, WS_SCH_*) show carried-forward constants: WS_SCH_H NGA identical for 2012-2023, WS_SCH_W_DW NGA identical for 2012-2023, WS_SCH_H BRA identical for 2011-2019, WS_HCF_W BRA identical 88.8849 for 2008-2016, WS_HCF_S BRA flat 2017-2021, WS_SCH_H IND flat 2016-2023; several others are flat for the first 5 years then strictly linear (modeled extrapolation, not survey points).
6. WS_PPL_S_OD (BRA): literal 0 for 2022-2024; WS_PPL_W_DW (BRA) 2024 = 99.99999873230279 (model ceiling).
7. WS_PPL_W_SW (BRA): series ends in 2016 whereas NGA and IND run to 2024.
8. Many JMP values carry 14-16 significant digits (unrounded model output); stored verbatim as returned.
9. PT_CHLD_LBR_ECON (IND) 2024 = 0.9 is a single point and low relative to other published estimates; flag for source check.
10. Several nutrition variables (NT_ANT_WHZ_*, NT_BF_EXBF, NT_CF_MMF, NT_IOD_*) list an alternative facet 676179467171518898 (observationPeriod P1M) for NGA and/or IND; not queried (only the default facet was fetched).
11. Operational: the shared scratchpad helper script was overwritten by another process mid-run (first append attempt timed out); re-created under a private subdirectory and the affected batch (NT_ANT_WHZ_NE3) was re-appended once, with no duplicate rows (verified: 117 unique (variable,country) pairs, 1,084 rows).
